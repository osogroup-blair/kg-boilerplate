# Code of conduct

We want this to be a welcoming place to learn and build. This code is short, in the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers; explain instead of sneering at a question.
- Give and accept feedback graciously, and credit others' work.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing others' private information.
- Keep the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) on topic and free of spam.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who do not follow this code. To report a problem, use GitHub's *Report content* on the comment, or contact the maintainers through a private security advisory if the matter is sensitive. Reports are handled in confidence.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
